Instructions and Questions

How to heat your warmer, keep it clean, and enjoy it safely.

Heating and care

  1. Heat

    Neck warmers: Fold your rice bag with the flannelette cover in half and place on a microwavable plate. Microwave on high for 30 seconds, flip and heat in 5-10 second intervals until warm.
    Hand warmers: Heat hand warmers in 5-10 second intervals until warm. Microwaves vary, so start low.

  2. Check

    Test the warmth on your hand before it touches your neck, and give it a squeeze to spread the heat evenly. Neck and hand warmers should be gently warm to touch, never hot!

  3. Enjoy

    Drape the neck warmer over your neck and shoulders, place on any aching muscles or just use it to snuggle with. Hold hand warmers in your hands on a cold winter day or when you just need some warm hands. Reheat as needed.

  4. Wash and store

    Remove the flannelette cover on the neck warmer bag. Machine wash it on a cold and gentle cycle. Line or tumble dry with low heat. Never wash or soak the rice-filled inner bag. Note: Because the hand warmers do not have a removable cover, they can not be laundered. Spot clean as needed. Store the warmers in a cool and dry place.

Important information: Never leave a warmer heating unattended or use it while you sleep. If it smells scorched or if the rice bag fabric is damaged, stop using it. Warmers are for comfort, not medical treatment, and are not toys for small children or pets.

Common questions

What are the warmers filled with?

Our warmers are filled with rice (usually scented Jasmin) and sewn into an inner bag, which sits inside a removable cover.

What are the covers made of?

Our covers are made with 100% cotton, usually flannelette, which is very soft and cozy. The fabric selection changes from batch to batch.

Do the hand warmers match the neck warmers?

Yes. Every fabric is sewn as a neck warmer cover with two matching hand warmers.

Is it safe to microwave the warmers?

Yes! Our warmers are made with 100% cotton fabric and thread, meaning that it is safe to use in the microwave. Make sure you follow the instructions to avoid over heating and burns. Beware of other fabrics and threads that may contain mixed materials which may NOT be safe to be microwaved.

How long does the warmth last?

That depends. If you follow our instructions, the neck warmers will last 20 to 30 minutes, and the hand warmers about 20 minutes when held in your hand. When it cools, simply heat it again!

Can I order from this website?

No. This site is for sharing our work. Warmers are sold in person at the craft fairs listed on the Craft Fairs page.
