Why a rice warmer?

Sewn by hand

Every neck warmer and hand warmer is uniquely handcrafted, meaning gentle variations in shape, size or colour make every single warmer one of a kind!

Removable cotton covers

The soft 100% cotton cover slips off for easy washing, so your warmer stays fresh season after season.

Gentle, lasting heat

Rice holds its warmth for about 20 to 30 minutes and settles to the shape of your neck or palm.

Choose your favourite fabric:

The washable covers are made from 100% cotton flannelette, offering a wonderfully soft, cozy feel in a variety of beautiful colours and patterns. Hand warmers are sewn to match every neck warmer.
